Penetration testing, infrastructure & Active Directory, and AI agent security.

I believe the best way to understand how a system can be attacked is to understand how it is built.

I build systems to understand their architecture and assumptions — how their components interact, where trust is established, and how data and authority move through them. Then I try to break them.

As a penetration tester, I apply this mindset to traditional applications and infrastructure. As I move deeper into AI and agent security, I'm interested in what changes when software can reason, use tools, retain context, and act on behalf of users.

This blog is where I document that process: security research, experiments, attack techniques, tooling, failures, and the lessons learned from trying to understand systems by building and breaking them.

Offensive security consultant with 5+ years of hands-on penetration testing and 2 years focused on AI agent security.

I break into web apps, infrastructure, and Active Directory environments, then turn what I find into risk that engineers and executives can act on. Increasingly I spend that same attacker mindset on the newer surface — RAG, MCP, and A2A — where AI agents inherit all of it. Currently working toward OSCP and CPTS.
