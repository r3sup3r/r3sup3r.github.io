Each post builds on the last: we start with the fundamentals of LLM architecture, move into real attacks (prompt injection, indirect injection, agent hijacking), then tackle specialized domains (code assistants, classifiers, browser agents), and finally ship production-ready assessment methodology. Everything is local-first—Ollama, no cloud APIs—and grounded in OWASP LLM Top 10 and MITRE ATLAS. I'm learning in real time. These are my notes.
