// creed

I believe the best way to understand how a system can be attacked is to understand how it is built.

I build systems to learn their architecture and assumptions: how components interact, where trust is established, and how data and authority move through the system. Then I try to break them.

As a penetration tester I apply that mindset to applications and infrastructure. Now I am applying it to AI and agent security, where software can reason, use tools, retain context, and act on behalf of users.
