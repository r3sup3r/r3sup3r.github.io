I believe the best way to understand how a system can be attacked is to understand how it is built.

I build systems to understand their architecture and assumptions: how components interact, where trust is established, and how data and authority move through the system. Then I try to break them.

As a penetration tester, I apply the same mindset to applications and infrastructure. Now I'm applying it to AI and agent security, where software can reason, use tools, retain context, and act on behalf of users.

This blog is where I document that process: security research, experiments, attack techniques, tooling, failures, and what I learn along the way.
